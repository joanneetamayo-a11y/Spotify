Quick guide

Topic: access = login; usability = controls, playlists or ads; playback = playing music, crashes or lag; downloads = saved/offline music; catalog = songs, discovery or lyrics; billing = charges, subscription or premium-only features; support = help response; other = general or unrelated comments.

Intent precedence: explicit leaving/cancelling → complaint → request without failure → praise → unclear. A refund request alone does not necessarily mean cancellation. Unrelated slogans do not imply personal departure.

Severity: 1 no problem; 2 annoyance or generic criticism; 3 restricted/degraded function; 4 clearly blocked core task; 5 explicit serious financial/privacy/data harm. Do not infer harm or a blocked task from anger alone.

If unsure or unable to interpret the language, choose “Needs review: Yes” and explain briefly. You may leave labels blank for such cases. Notes are optional otherwise.
